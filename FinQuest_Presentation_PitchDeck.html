Why Traditional Financial Education Fails Young Adults

Young adults leave colleges knowing calculus and history, yet enter an aggressive economy weaponized with instant credit traps, predatory algorithms, and frictionless UPI debt.

⚠️ The 42% APR Compounding Debt Trap

Credit card revolving balances and deceptive "No-Cost EMI" options charge up to 36–42% APR. A ₹30,000 festive shopping balance paid with only minimum dues takes over 8 years to clear and costs ₹48,000+ in pure interest.

🚨 Reverse UPI & Cyber Fraud Epidemic

Cyber fraud complaints in India surged past 1.5 million in 2025. Young users repeatedly fall for fake UPI refund QR codes, Telegram stock tips, and fake employment tasks, erasing entire liquid savings in seconds.

📉 Passive Learning Has Zero Retention

Boring YouTube seminars and textbook chapters do not create behavioral muscle memory. People only learn financial consequences when their own bank balance hits zero—a lesson that ruins lives in the real world.

🎯 The Core Mission of FinQuest under GD-01

To create an action-driven Financial Flight Simulator that lets young adults crash and recover in a high-fidelity virtual economy, teaching them mathematical compounding, debt defense, fraud recognition, and liquidity management before they risk a single real rupee.
